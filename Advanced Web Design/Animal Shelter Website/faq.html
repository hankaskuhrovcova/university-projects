Frequently Asked Questions

Welcome to Dublin Paws Animal Shelter. Below you’ll find answers to the most common questions about adopting, visiting, and supporting our shelter in Dublin, Ireland.

Adoption

Where are you located?

We are located at 73 Glasnevin, Dublin 9, Ireland.

What are your opening hours?

Monday–Friday: 10am–5pm
Saturday: 11am–4pm
Sunday: Closed

How does the adoption process work?

1. Fill out an application form
2. Meet the animal
3. Home check
4. Pay adoption fee and take your pet home

What is included in the adoption fee?

Vaccines, microchip, neutering/spaying, and full vet check.

Visiting the Shelter

Do I need an appointment?

No, you can walk in during public hours.

Can I bring my children?

Yes, but children must be supervised at all times.

Can I bring my dog to meet another dog?

Yes, but please contact us in advance to arrange this safely.

Volunteering & Donations

How can I volunteer?

You can apply through our Volunteer page and attend a short training session.

What donations do you accept?

Money, food, blankets, toys, leads, and pet beds.

Can I fundraise for the shelter?

Yes! Email us at fundraising@dublinpaws.ie to get started.

Animals in Our Care

What animals do you take in?

We care for a wide range of animals including dogs, cats, small animals, farm animals such as llamas, and occasionally exotic animals, depending on our capacity and specialist care availability.

Can I surrender my pet?

Yes, by appointment only. Please contact us first.

Do you offer fostering?

Yes, foster carers are always needed for young or sick animals.
