Volunteering & Fostering

Welcome to Dublin Paws Animal Shelter. We’re delighted that you’re considering volunteering or fostering with us. Together, we can give vulnerable animals the care, safety, and second chances they deserve.

Volunteers and Foster Carers play a vital role in the daily life of Dublin Paws. From hands-on animal care to enrichment, socialisation, and support, our volunteers help prepare animals for loving forever homes. We are incredibly grateful for the time, compassion, and dedication shared by our volunteer community.

In addition to individual volunteering, Dublin Paws works with local businesses and organisations through Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) initiatives, offering meaningful team-building opportunities that directly support animal welfare.

Exciting news: Dublin Paws will soon be expanding our corporate volunteering and fostering programmes. New opportunities will be announced soon — watch this space!

How to become a volunteer

Volunteering with Dublin Paws Animal Shelter is a meaningful way to make a real difference in the lives of animals in need. Our volunteers are an essential part of our day-to-day work, helping us provide care, enrichment, and compassion to every animal that comes through our doors.

Becoming a volunteer is a step-by-step process designed to ensure both our volunteers and animals feel safe, supported, and confident. From choosing the right role to completing training and joining our volunteer team, we’ll guide you every step of the way.

Below you’ll find everything you need to know about the process, commitment, and training involved in volunteering with Dublin Paws.

Let’s get started

Find the role that suits you

Explore our volunteer roles to see what’s involved in each position. Please note that, with the exception of events, all volunteering roles take place on-site at the Dublin Paws Animal Shelter.

Commitment

Volunteers must be able to commit to a minimum of 8 hours per month for at least 6 months.

Apply

You must be 18+ to volunteer with Dublin Paws. Complete an application for the role you are interested in. Due to a high number of applications, our team will be in touch if you are shortlisted.

Interview

Successful applicants will be contacted by our volunteer team for a short chat so we can get to know you better.

Nearly there

Training

New volunteers must complete 3 training sessions. Training is hands-on with the animals, so please dress for all types of weather and don’t mind getting a little dirty.

Completion of training sessions does not guarantee a volunteer role.

Become a volunteer

Successful trainees will then be ready to begin volunteering with Dublin Paws. Upon acceptance, there is an administrative fee of €20 to help cover programme costs.

This includes a Dublin Paws volunteer t-shirt and access to our volunteer management app.
